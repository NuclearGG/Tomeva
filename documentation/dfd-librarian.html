Librarian Interface — Local-First Operations

The Electron desktop app the librarian uses at the front desk. Every issue, return, and record change happens against the local LokiJS database first — the cloud is only ever a once-an-hour summary going out, and a few small signals coming back in.

External Entity — a person or outside data source
Process — an action the app performs
Data Store — where information is kept
Reading this diagram: arrows show the direction data moves, animated to make the rhythm of the sync legible — a steady hourly heartbeat out to the cloud, and continuous local activity underneath it. Dashed motion pauses automatically if your system prefers reduced motion.
