Libra · Data Flow Diagram

Admin Interface — Institutional Visibility

The browser dashboard for the Principal, Vice Principal, and school staff. Everything here reads from the same hourly snapshot the librarian pushes — nothing here can issue, return, or edit a book directly. The one exception is identity: staff accounts are the only ones trusted to grant students access.

External Entity — a person or outside data source
Process — an action the app performs
Data Store — where information is kept
Gate, not gatekeeper for data: Process 1 (Authenticate) is a control check, not a data flow — it verifies the sign-in email ends in @meacademy.in before Processes 3 and 4 (which can write) become usable. Processes 2 and 5 are read/notify only and don't require the staff-domain check.
Reading this diagram: the thick, colored arrows are the flows that actually change data (suspend toggle, authorizing a student). Thin, dark arrows are reads or two-way messaging. Motion pauses automatically if your system prefers reduced motion.
