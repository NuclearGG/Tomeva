A note from the Tomeva Project

Your data's safety
is our Priority Zero.

Legacy downloads are temporarily paused.

We've identified security concerns in the older technology used by Tomeva Legacy.

While we work on a safer alternative, we've temporarily stopped downloads for Windows 7 and Windows 8.1.

We believe protecting your library's records and your students' information is more important than releasing software before we're confident in its safety.

We're working on bringing Tomeva Legacy back with a safer, lightweight foundation.

Thank you for your patience and understanding.

— The Tomeva Project

A little more clarity

Questions you may have.

Why can't I download Tomeva Legacy?

We've temporarily paused downloads while we address security concerns in the older technology used by this edition.

Has my library's data been compromised?

We have not identified evidence of a compromise from this issue. The suspension is a precaution to reduce potential risks.

Will Tomeva Legacy return?

We're developing a safer, lightweight alternative for older Windows computers. We haven't announced a release date yet.

Can I still use modern Tomeva?

Yes. Downloads of the supported Modern Edition remain available for Windows 10 and Windows 11.

What if I already installed Tomeva Legacy?

We recommend avoiding online features in the older edition. Keep a secure backup of your library records and consult the project's security guidance before continuing to use it.
